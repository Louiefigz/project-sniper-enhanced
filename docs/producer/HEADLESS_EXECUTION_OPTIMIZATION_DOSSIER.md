## What “over 95% sure” means

Do not manufacture one percentage from reviewer opinion or average correlated red/green domains with subjective weights. The defensible target is a **95%-controlled release decision** for one frozen claim:

> For this frozen deterministic-MP4 quality-pass release, conditional on pre-feedback initialization and the declared target population/policy/build, the actual portfolio meets its absolute SLA, is at least 65% faster than its frozen comparator, is quality-noninferior, and its full-job qualified-success probability is greater than 95%.

Editable Palmier is outside that claim until its remote ambiguity and QC blockers close. “This is universally the right architecture” has no natural sampling unit and receives no percentage.

Unless a broader creator-sampling plan is preregistered, the first claim is explicitly labeled **system-ready automation latency and reliability for Aaron's first organic quality pass, conditional on initialization before feedback**. It does not cover initial-short planning/build speed, request-to-Aaron-decision workflow time, or later passes. Initial creation/initialization require separate cohorts; report human-decision elapsed and operator seconds separately. Failed initializations cannot disappear through successful-initialization-only enrollment. Freeze only pre-feedback immutable inclusion criteria plus calendar/load policy; effect eligibility, outages, and rate limits are outcomes. Projects across days remain one-creator evidence. Future generalization additionally requires a frozen stationary distribution, numeric drift/expiry rules, and cohort reset on learning trend/provider degradation/build or mix drift; otherwise report only the observed calendar interval.

Freeze two non-conflicting outcome definitions before data collection. `QualifiedComparisonJobV1` applies identically to both confirmatory arms and requires exact request/effect fulfillment, the arm's predeclared full-quality policy, Audit B plus checked full decode, every required critic/evidence-access receipt, no unplanned intervention, correct generation publication inside the common headless wrapper, a sealed required trace, completion within the absolute SLA horizon `H`, and the same blinded absolute human acceptance policy. `QualifiedHeadlessJobSuccessV1` is the treatment-only reliability outcome and may add frozen treatment routing/fallback obligations but cannot weaken that shared definition. Timeout, malformed/missing evidence, recovery beyond the SLA, or post-enrollment exclusion is failure.

## Non-negotiable model

### Source truth, realization, and delivery are separate

Never infer construction or delivery from where source truth came from.

- `sourceAuthority`: filesystem manifest/transcript/media snapshot, or a Palmier project/timeline/readback fingerprint.
- `realizationKind`: how content is constructed—initially `deterministic-mp4` or `palmier-editable`.
- `deliveries[]`: sinks that distribute the exact same approved realization bytes, such as `local-file` or `palmier-flat`.

`palmier-flat` is an MP4 delivery, not a third realization. A Palmier card repair creates a new generation of a `palmier-editable` realization, not a delivery. The first headless API v1 release allows only deterministic MP4 plus local-file output; Palmier is disabled. Any later multi-delivery contract allows one realization per job, and multiple deliveries may distribute only the same immutable approved bytes. Identical bytes do not make a remote delivery exactly-once.

Only the approved cut is currently proven target-neutral. The existing `edit_plan.json` contains renderer kinds, pixel geometry, MP4 audio presets, transitions, crops, and renderer-specific profiles; treat it as `LegacyMp4PlanApprovalV1`, not as a portable editorial contract. Do not invent a broader target-neutral schema until another realization proves which semantics are actually shared.

Do not support ambiguous `both`, silently fall back from editable Palmier to MP4, or let ambient Palmier workspace state override an explicit MP4 realization. An explicit MP4 execution must not inspect or mutate Palmier.

### Authority roots

Use a small explicit authority graph. A generic filesystem Merkle DAG is not required.

1. `InputSourceRoot`: pre-plan immutable source media bytes, transcript, normalized manifest/catalog identity, intent-independent reference inputs, and declared source assets; no target or delivery fields.
2. `EditorialIntentRoot`: requested editorial outcome; no renderer, executor, or delivery mechanism.
3. `CutApprovalRoot`: target-neutral approved cuts against source and intent.
4. `AssetClosureRoot`: post-plan exact closure of every selected B-roll, music, graphic/template dependency, font, remote asset snapshot, and runtime input needed by the realization. An immutable content-addressed catalog may satisfy this without copying the whole asset library.
5. `RealizationApprovalRoot`: one realization kind, capability profile, the selected asset-closure root, and compiled renderer/executor instructions. The legacy plan enters here until a portable plan schema is proved.
6. `RenderReceiptRoot`: exact upstream roots, implementation/toolchain identities, and output hashes.
7. `QcReceiptRoot`: exact final bytes, exact audited-plan hash, audit/frame/audio policy, critic configuration, and verdict artifacts.
8. `FinalApprovalV3`: one realization generation and the exact roots and output it approves.

Every stage receipt must include its schema, stage, exact compiled input projection, implementation digest, toolchain identity, output SHA-256, output size/media facts, and receipt digest. Editorial-approval roots and renderer-stage roots remain separate. Missing dependencies are explicit failures, never silently omitted fields. The reproduced failure and design rule are documented in [EDITORIAL_HASHES_ARE_NOT_RENDER_DAGS.md](../../scripts/producer/docs/findings/EDITORIAL_HASHES_ARE_NOT_RENDER_DAGS.md).

For the normal local threat model, a coherent flat sorted `{path, sha256}` manifest is adequate. Local signatures do not protect against a hostile process running as the same user when the signing key and verifier are available to that user. Cross-host signing is a separate security decision.

### Planning/execution boundary and generations

Do not start with a monolithic JobV2, a branch inside the existing detached V1 worker, or independently scheduled sinks. Add one dedicated async `HeadlessMp4AttemptV1` protocol and one MP4-only composition root. Existing Auto Edit/AI Edit routes, V1 jobs, run registry, root status/quality markers, and GUI readers stay on a separate legacy branch and are only the disposable comparator. The initial headless release makes no compatibility projection and does not transparently migrate the GUI branch.

The new CLI/controller composition root parses a closed `ExecutionPolicyV1` before any saved-plan shortcut, Palmier classifier, or Palmier guard. Static and transitive dependency tests must prove that its TypeScript bundle and Python import closure contain no Palmier module. Runtime tripwires fail any Palmier file, socket, MCP/tool, mirror, checkpoint, invalidation, import, retry, or drain attempt.

Frozen headless attempts compute in unique private generation directories under resource admission and per-cache-key exclusion. They do not hold the authority publisher lock for their runtime. Only the short publish step takes a kernel-backed lock on a never-unlinked inode, verifies stable identity, expected parent, and an independently rotated durable `FENCE`, revalidates exact bytes, and advances `CURRENT`. Expected-parent comparison alone cannot stop a cancelled stale worker when the parent has not changed.

Planning approval and execution completion remain separate facts inside one durable headless quality-pass attempt. Stage retries may consume the same immutable realization digest without changing its approval, but the first controller operation chains planning/repair through render/QC/publication and returns one terminal pinned result. A later split executor is optional and requires its own durable candidate handoff.

Minimum durable layout. Project identity is a project-local `.sniper/lineage.json` created under a dedicated never-unlinked lineage/authority-create kernel lock, not a legacy root-writer lease. `authorityId` is the domain-separated hash of `{schemaVersion, lineageId, realizationKind}` and is never a path, name, run, or attempt token. A filesystem copy becomes a permitted isolated branch even if it duplicates the lineage UUID because authority storage remains project-local; it never counts independently or auto-merges. “Conflicting/multiply claimed” means incompatible records within one canonical root, not two explicit copied roots:

```text
<project>/.sniper/authorities/<authorityId>/
```

Generation commits bind stable lineage, realization, generation, attempt/request/unit identity, expected parent, policies, and upstream roots. Fence token and proposed publication sequence live in durable publish intent and publisher validation, not immutable generation content; `CURRENT` records the resulting sequence. Mutable attempt/publish/recovery state never lives inside the sealed generation. `CURRENT` contains `{authorityId, publicationSeq, generationId, commitDigest}`; `FENCE` separately contains `{fenceRevision, fenceToken, activeAttemptId}`. Admission permits one publisher-capable attempt per authority. `CURRENT` advances only after complete verification and while the short-held publisher compares full parent plus exact fence token. An unlocked read followed by rename is not compare-and-swap. No root or GUI projection exists initially.

For MP4, media, proof, QC approval, and `commit.json` live in one generation. Allocate the final UUID path before work but expose only attempt staging to writers. Reap writer-capable descendants; a trusted sealer copies/verified-reflinks into fresh final inodes; sandboxed read-only verifiers write outside final; then the trusted controller writes relative receipts/approvals, writes `commit.json` last, flushes, and makes the generation read-only. `FinalApprovalV3` may contain `generationId` but never the future commit digest; `commit.json` lists every authoritative relative generation file except itself and excludes attempt state/trace/log/cache/work/temp/locks/pointers. Large external CAS objects require validated digests plus durable reachability pins. `CURRENT` is the visibility boundary. The initial claim is process-crash/SIGKILL and server-restart atomicity, not universal hardware power-loss durability.

Every reachable generation pins its external source/asset/tool CAS objects. Missing/substituted/digest-mismatched objects make authority `BROKEN`; never refetch mutable remote bytes. Generation and external-CAS GC are both disabled until joint reachability, reader pins, refcounts, and crash recovery are proved.

Readers pin one validated `{authorityId, publicationSeq, generationId, commitDigest}` once per operation and resolve every plan/media/proof/approval read relative to that generation. They never re-read `CURRENT` halfway through one operation. With automatic GC disabled, concurrent publication may advance the pointer without invalidating the pinned reader.

For Palmier, order project admission before the user-global Palmier singleton broker and persist an operation intent before every external side effect, including flat delivery. Bind each local intent to an operation key, expected parent, authority, attempt/fence, and capability identity; read back and path-diff the exact visible result before persisting its receipt. The current MCP surface does not send a server-enforced idempotency key or remote expected-parent CAS, so a local fence cannot prevent an already-sent stale effect. It can only prevent local approval, activation through the broker, or promotion. Classify failure as pre-send safe, definitive rejection, or ambiguous post-send. An ambiguous candidate quarantines and blocks only that Palmier target; it must not freeze unrelated local MP4 work. No blind retry, queue drain, speculative cleanup, or canonical activation is allowed.

Open each plan, manifest, transcript, media, and asset once through a safe allowlisted descriptor under the declared admission/read lock or filesystem snapshot/reflink contract, copy/hash from that descriptor, and parse only the frozen snapshot. Check pre/post `fstat` identity, size, and modification metadata and reject in-place truncate, append, or same-inode rewrite during capture. Approval, translation, and execution must consume the same frozen bytes. Root paths, URLs/protocols, symlinks, devices, FIFOs, sockets, and arbitrary absolute plan paths do not enter generation authority.

Executors never overwrite an approved plan. Separate `ProjectIdentityContext` from `GenerationExecutionContext`; the current overloaded `ctx.dir` cannot be redirected safely. `AdmissionInputsV1` freezes parent/source/feedback/request identity/operator/template policy/toolchain/execution policy. Post-planning `RealizationInputsV1` freezes `ResolvedPlanV1`, controller effect/routing, repair/fallback disposition/state, cut approval, selected asset closure, and exact template approval/history before render. `GenerationInputsV1` binds both. `GenerationPathsV1` contains only committed artifacts; `AttemptPathsV1` contains mutable state/publish intent/trace/log/cache/work/temp/staging. Render and assemble receive explicit neutral template authority. All normalization/recompose/refit occurs before lint/template approval/critics and freezes exact execution-plan bytes; any later change is a new unapproved candidate. See [APPROVE_THE_RESOLVED_PLAN_NOT_THE_INPUT_PLAN.md](../../scripts/producer/docs/findings/APPROVE_THE_RESOLVED_PLAN_NOT_THE_INPUT_PLAN.md).

The headless worker runs from one sealed controller/runtime closure: pinned Node/tsx/Python/ffmpeg/ffprobe/HyperFrames/browser/fonts/models and vendored GSAP, with attempt-owned temp/cache/work roots. Model processes receive read-only evidence and emit schema-validated stdout; the controller alone writes candidates. Claude uses safe mode, strict empty MCP, no Chrome/slash commands/session resume, explicit closed tools, and pinned skill bytes; Codex uses read-only/no-tools structured output or an OS sandbox and ignores ambient config/rules/apps/hooks/multi-agent behavior. A bundle-metafile denylist, Python AST import-closure test, runtime-capability manifest/offline smokes, and runtime tripwires prove the boundary.

Initialization cold-builds and fully requalifies generation zero from one presealed content-addressed legacy snapshot captured from an immutable clone/CAS or under a declared quiescent-capture contract. It never reads a mutable root set as an atomic snapshot or imports the absolute-path V2 approval. Authority state is explicit: `UNINITIALIZED`, `INITIALIZING`, `READY`, or `BROKEN`; absent `CURRENT` is valid only in the first two proved states and prior publish evidence forbids expected-null overwrite. After initialization, headless reads only committed generations; corruption/mismatch fails closed with no root fallback. Existing GUI/root readers remain blind. Automatic generation GC is disabled initially.

Attempt phase and disposition are orthogonal. Every `SUCCEEDED/FAILED/BLOCKED/STALE/CANCELED` outcome reaches `TERMINAL_SEALED` before the authority's active attempt clears. Publish intent pins attempt/request, parent, fence token, generation/commit, and result tuple before `CURRENT`; no later admission occurs until recovery seals it. A crash after pointer publication recovers only that exact tuple and never downgrades committed authority to failure.

## Optimization order

### Phase 0 — measure the complete job and reserve capacity

First freeze and hash the target population, route/effect class, fallback, comparator, `QualifiedComparisonJobV1`, `QualifiedHeadlessJobSuccessV1`, absolute SLA horizon `H`, quality margins/denominators, code/runtime/model identity, exclusions, numeric economic ceilings, and statistical look/stop rules. ## Release gates

### Deterministic gates

- Any source, B-roll, music, asset, transcript, manifest, intent, or reference change invalidates exactly the affected receipts.
- Parsed, hashed, approved, and executed bytes come from one frozen descriptor-backed/admission-locked snapshot; A→B swaps, in-place truncate/append/same-size rewrite during capture, symlinks, devices, URLs, and out-of-root paths fail.
- The dedicated headless CLI/controller root parses the closed execution policy before any work. Its build graph and Python import closure contain no Palmier module; runtime tripwires prove zero Palmier network attempts, reads, probes, MCP/tool calls, checkpoints, approval invalidations, imports, mirrors, retries, or drains.
- Replacing cached output bytes without a matching receipt fails.
- Cross-realization fresh retry cannot reuse another realization approval.
- Existing V1 routes/jobs cannot read, archive, resume, default, overwrite, or publish a headless attempt or authority. Headless never interprets V1 job state.
- Cancel first checks publication under the lock. If `CURRENT` equals the attempt's publish-intent tuple, publication wins and recovery seals success; otherwise cancel rotates/flushes the independent fence before process termination. A stale attempt cannot publish when `CURRENT` is unchanged. Ambiguous Palmier effects quarantine only that Palmier target.
- Resume executes the originally attested controller/runtime or fails closed.
- Crashes at every local write, MP4 commit, Palmier fork, operation, QC, and promotion boundary cannot create false approval. Palmier ambiguity produces zero blind retries and remains unpromoted unless the server supplies enough identity for exact reconciliation.
- Process-crash and `ENOSPC` injection before/after each authoritative write, required flush, commit, and pointer update yields the old valid generation or the complete new one—never a mixture. Hardware power-loss is a separate platform qualification.
- Publication and recovery revalidate the sealed generation manifest and exact bytes before accepting or advancing `CURRENT`; post-verification mutation fails.
- Every reader pins one validated commit tuple for its full operation; a concurrent `CURRENT` advance cannot mix plan/media/proof/approval generations.
- Initialization consumes one presealed content-addressed legacy snapshot, cold-builds and requalifies generation zero, and publishes expected-null. Concurrent initialization, an existing valid `CURRENT`, truncated/missing pointer, missing generation, or snapshot drift follows a closed idempotency/failure contract with no root fallback.
- Attempt completion and every approval result derive from the exact committed generation plus required delivery receipts; the terminal response pins that tuple and never lazily resolves a later `CURRENT`.
- Every Palmier mutation is explained by a compiled plan element and journaled intent/receipt.
- Every Desktop Pre reservation is atomic and every Post matches operation/session/args/parent/fence, not only tool name.
- Concurrent shared-cache misses publish one verified object through per-key exclusion and unique staging.
- A bounded repair uses stable target identity plus exact parent hash and expected old value. Its normalized JSON-pointer diff is a subset of its controller-owned effect allowlist; protected lane hashes, source/runtime roots, and derived artifacts such as `cover.png` remain exact. A no-op/identical replay succeeds only when request lineage and effect proof bind the current commit to that exact request; stale parent/old value is `STALE` even if visible value matches.
- Candidate failure cannot clear, overwrite, or make unrecoverable the previously approved generation. Only a fully proved candidate may replace it through locked expected-parent compare-and-swap.
- Candidate rendering cannot rename, rewrite, refit, or delete any file reachable from the selected parent generation. Base, plan, refit, MP4, proof, cover, diagnostics, reviews, and approval are one immutable generation manifest; the initial release creates no root compatibility projection.
- Recovery separates phase `ADMITTED/RUNNING/VERIFIED/PUBLISH_INTENT/POINTER_COMMITTED/TERMINAL_SEALED` from disposition `PENDING/SUCCEEDED/FAILED/BLOCKED/STALE/CANCELED`. Every outcome seals before the active fence clears. A kill before/after pointer publication resolves from the exact publish intent and never reconstructs truth by rewriting backups.
- A rejected candidate and an in-progress repair have different durable identities. Stop/restart before repair promotion resumes or restarts the repair from its immutable parent; it cannot rerun Audit B/critics against the same rejected candidate and charge the same repair twice.
- The forced-full oracle begins without a base, fingerprint, work product, or resume state, invokes the direct full-base entry point, and proves every expected full stage occurred after attempt start. Fingerprint deletion or an auto-refit/reuse branch is not a valid control.
- Every started child in a parallel batch reaches a recorded terminal/reaped state before the parent returns, including first-error and cancellation paths.
- Every daemon-owned resource has a parent-allocated exact identity durably
  registered before launch. Success/cancel prove exact absence before clearing
  the record. Startup recovery additionally proves that no prior creator can
  still act, and binds creator/boot, Docker engine, container ID/labels, and the
  current fence. An absence window alone never clears or reuses a row.
- Audit infrastructure faults, evidence faults, and plan-quality defects have distinct typed outcomes; only a proved plan defect may enter a plan-repair loop.
- Experiment cleanup is manifest-driven beneath the attempt root, rejects symlinks/globs/empty paths/CAS or active-lease targets, runs only after exported receipt verification, and appends a cleanup tombstone.
- Live locks cannot be stolen by age; process identity includes start/boot identity plus the exact fence revision/token.
- Authority and lock permissions remain restrictive under a permissive umask, and symlink/nonregular substitution cannot redirect writes.
