# Training pairs — viewer knows the problem

Each pair shows retained source words (Input) and an opening built only from them
(Output). The viewer already feels the problem; the opening names it precisely.

### T001
Input: "so every time I send a quote the client just goes quiet, and I used to think they
were ghosting me, but it was the first line of the quote, it opened with my price"
Output: "Clients go quiet after your quote? Check the first line."
Why it works: names the exact symptom the viewer has, and promises a concrete cause.

### T002
Input: "the second week is where people drop off, week one is excitement, week two is
where you actually need a system"
Output: "Week two is where every new habit dies."
Why it works: a specific, recognisable moment, stated as the speaker said it.

### T003
Input: "I was editing for two hours a video, two hours, and most of that time was spent
finding the good takes, not cutting them"
Output: "Two hours editing, and most of it was finding takes."
Why it works: the number and the surprising split are both in the source.

### T004
Input: "if you are rewriting your proposal from scratch every single week you are
wasting the one hour you actually have for sales"
Output: "Rewriting your proposal every week? That's your sales hour."
Why it works: calls out a repeated situation and attaches the real cost.

### T005
Input: "nobody listens to the audio before they record, they check the camera five
times and the mic zero times"
Output: "You check the camera five times. The mic, zero."
Why it works: contrast the viewer will recognise, kept in the speaker's numbers.

### T006
Input: "the reason your posts don't get replies is they end on a statement, there is
nothing to answer"
Output: "Your posts end on a statement. Nothing to answer."
Why it works: diagnoses a cause the viewer did not know they had.

### T007
Input: "I thought I needed more clients but I needed fewer clients who paid properly"
Output: "I didn't need more clients. I needed fewer, better-paying ones."
Why it works: a reversal of the viewer's assumption, in the speaker's own terms.

### T008
Input: "the busiest weeks were my worst weeks for money because I was doing all the
small jobs I never charged enough for"
Output: "My busiest weeks made the least money. Here's why."
Why it works: a paradox that the rest of the Short resolves.
