# Finish & export in Palmier Pro

The editor renders a finished video on its own — but when you want to hand-polish, color-grade, or export through a real timeline, that's what **Palmier Pro** is for.

## How it works

1. Let Sniper finish its render and QC; Palmier does not need to stay open.
2. When you approve the cut, use **Update Palmier mirror**. The approved
   `final.mp4` is published exactly and placed as one flat clip.
3. Use Palmier for clip-level finishing, color, or export. Any new Palmier
   changes create a newer working revision and should be reviewed before final
   delivery.

## Good to know

- The AI's render is the **source of truth**. Palmier is where you *finish*, not where the edit is rebuilt from scratch.
- Graphics, captions, motion, transitions, and audio arrive baked into that
  one clip exactly as approved; their internal controls are not reconstructed.
- A native editable/hybrid build is available only as an isolated experiment.
  Its local candidate/QC/promotion protocol is wired, but representative
  connected short/long qualification is still open, so it remains P5-blocked.
- Palmier is **optional** — if the AI's finished video is all you need, you're already done.
