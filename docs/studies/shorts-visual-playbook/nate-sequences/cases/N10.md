**Opening:** A ClickUp identity graphic becomes a crossed-out grid of other apps above the presenter. The visible contradiction supplies a reason to investigate the replacement claim. It is a promotional claim, not proven by the opening itself.

**Payoff:** The source shows several useful interfaces and artifacts, including a visible request-to-response chain. Most broad replacement and autonomous-work claims remain illustrative; the investor prompt and following deck are different examples.
