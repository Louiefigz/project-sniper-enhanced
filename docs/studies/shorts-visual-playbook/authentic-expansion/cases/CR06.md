# CR06 · Numbered questions without leaving the interview

Version `2026-09-10.3` · [Original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL5_Dicy28d/) · 54.208s

**Message:** Work backward from a desired outcome to what you must be known for, do and learn.

**Story:** Four-question promise → questions in order → explanation of aimless activity → intentional direction → medium choice.

**Format foundation:** A short ordinal marker can orient a list without forcing a full diagram or a permanent four-box layout. The source keeps facial expression and explanatory speech as the primary information.

**Good adaptation:** Make the current question easy to follow, then clear its ordinal. Keep the face large enough for the personal explanation. Preserve the change from framework to analogy and conclusion. Use our readable brand typography instead of copying this reel's gold serif.

**Limits and counterexample:** Do not use a new template for every question. Do not leave all four questions plus word captions on screen if they compete for reading. The later passage opens a new topic, so a standalone edit needs a deliberate endpoint.

**Text roles:** Gold serif phrase captions with shadow; larger gold #2/#3/#4 briefly replace speech captions. Quoted dialogue appears italic. These are observed role distinctions, not a required font family.

**Compare:** CR07, A06. See [Format foundations](../../FORMAT_FOUNDATIONS.md).

**Possible mechanisms:** `native-presenter-crop`, `native-captions`, `titlecard-calm`. These require configuration/composition and review; they are not rendered matches.
