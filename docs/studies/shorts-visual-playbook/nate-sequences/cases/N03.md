**Lesson:** A short identity source can be reused as concrete output variations: preserve the same person while changing outfit or setting, compare variants for reuse, and show the specific constraint solved by studio-looking content while away.

**Opening:** The spoken hook claims this AI cloned the creator in fifteen seconds. A face mesh, scan line and obscured logo turn the claim into an identity-capture puzzle; the later HeyGen reveal names the mechanism. The scan is a visual metaphor and the actual input/output comparison must carry the demonstration.

**Story:** Identity hook/metaphorical scan → explanatory narrator → actual short source excerpt → name tool → compare narrator with claimed twin → isolate outfit then background changes → show new speaking output and avoided camera step → explain successful-content variations → show three versions → explain travel constraint with two-setting comparison → give output face space for fidelity claim → broaden to two different creator examples → brand/keyword CTA.

**Payoff:** Viewers can see the same-person continuity across different clothing/settings and understand why reuse or away-from-studio production is useful. Actual generation time, source lineage, voice fidelity, hook-test outcomes and realism are not independently verified by this sampled visual study.

**Why study this case:** The strongest identity/state-continuity example in this assignment: a nearly four-second simultaneous comparison, widening wardrobe/background reveals, three output bands, a travel-versus-studio contrast, literal avoided-camera B-roll and a held close face for detail evaluation.

**Review limits:** Viewed all 4 chronological 0.5-second context pages (77 samples including final frame) plus 90 framing samples on 10 extra strips. Original machine-caption grouping supplies spoken ideas; no audio audition or phonetic verification. Caption final word overruns video; source was not padded or retimed. Strip timestamps are ffmpeg eight-fps resampling estimates, not source-frame measurements. Continuous motion, exact typography, easing, generation provenance and product claims are not independently verified. Catalog source inspection supports possible mechanisms only; no rendered match or ready replica is claimed. One additional original-resolution opening frame at approximately 0.1 seconds was inspected to distinguish the face mesh, pixelated logo and arrow.
