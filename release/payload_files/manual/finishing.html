Finishing somewhere else (optional)

You do not need another editor. If you want one, here is exactly what does and does not survive the trip.

This whole page is optional. Sniper's approved final.mp4 is a finished, postable video. If that is all you need, you are already done.

Taking the video into any editor

Import producer/final.mp4 into Premiere, Final Cut, Resolve, CapCut or anything else. It is an ordinary H.264 MP4 with AAC audio.

What you are importing is one flat video. The graphics, captions, motion, transitions and audio are rendered into the picture. Your editor will not see separate caption tracks, editable text or individual graphic layers — there is nothing in an MP4 that could carry them. Anyone who tells you a flattened export preserves layers is wrong about the file format, not about Sniper.

So: use another editor for footage-level work — colour, a music swap, topping and tailing, stitching it into a longer piece. Do not plan to retype a caption there. Change the caption in Sniper and rebuild; that takes less time.

If you need the graphics editable, do it before you export

The editable graphics layer lives in Sniper, not downstream. Use the Studio review lane, then rebuild. That is the supported way to get a different graphics result.

Clipper: a real editable timeline for Final Cut

If what you want is a frame-accurate timeline rather than a flat video, that is what Clipper is for. It tightens one clip word by word and exports FCPXML, which Final Cut Pro opens as an actual timeline with your cuts as edits.

Segmenter: rough clips for your own editing

Segmenter splits a long recording into clips and does not re-encode anything, so there is no quality loss. The trade-off is that clips snap to the nearest keyframe at or before the intended start, so adjacent clips can overlap by a frame or two. That is intentional: they are raw material for you to trim, not finished cuts.

Palmier Pro

not configured in this package

Sniper can hand an approved video to Palmier Pro — a separate paid NLE — as one flat clip containing exactly the approved bytes. The qualified path is that one exact-copy mirror, and nothing more.

  • A native, layer-by-layer editable Palmier build exists only as an isolated experiment inside the source. It is not released and is not something to buy Sniper for.
  • This package does not configure the Palmier connection. install/doctor.command reports it as gated.
  • Palmier is a separate purchase from a different company.
