# LM05 · Personal montage with a text-led narrative

Version `2026-09-10.3` · [Original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcZPZuJxno1/) · 14.070s

**Message:** The creator says he left a prestigious role despite a record of accomplishments.

**Story:** Departure tension → claimed accomplishments → invitation to another post for the reason.

**Format foundation:** The person is not required to deliver the explanation on camera here. Personal footage supplies identity, place and emotional context while text carries the narrative. Text can persist across multiple shots, separating the reading rhythm from the cut rhythm.

**Good adaptation:** Use relevant owned personal footage and one readable thought at a time. Maintain text position while footage changes. If our Short promises a reason, give the reason in the Short; a related-post CTA can follow it.

**Limits and counterexample:** This source defers the answer to another post. It is a useful promotional pattern and a weak model for a self-contained teaching payoff. Travel footage is context, not documentary proof of financial or consulting claims.

**Text roles:** Short upper-center white text opens the montage, followed by black text on white rounded backing. The message persists over several underlying shots.

**Compare:** CR05, N09. See [Format foundations](../../FORMAT_FOUNDATIONS.md).

**Possible mechanisms:** `native-media`, `titlecard-calm`. These require configuration/composition and review; they are not rendered matches.
