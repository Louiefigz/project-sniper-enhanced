# What is Project Sniper?

Project Sniper is an AI-powered editing pipeline that takes your raw recordings and produces finished video — without you spending hours on a timeline. You tell it what you want; it makes the creative calls and renders the result.

It's really **four tools that share one workspace**, so you can go from a long raw recording all the way to a polished, captioned, ready-to-post cut.

## The four tools

### 🎛️ Producer — the main event
Add raw footage, pick what you want on a single card, and the AI authors and renders the whole edit: dead-air and filler removed, tighter pacing, vertical reframing, burned-in captions, hook cards, graphics, motion, and clean audio. Make a **Short (9:16)** or a **Long (16:9)**.

### 🎬 Segmenter
Got a two-hour recording? Segmenter rough-cuts it into individual clips you can actually work with — transcribe, let the AI find the segments, tweak the boundaries, export.

### ✂️ Clipper
Refine a single clip into a tight cut. The AI trims filler word-by-word; you fine-tune in a word editor and export a timeline for Final Cut Pro.

### 🔍 Frame Review
A fast quality check: scan a finished video for on-screen text mistakes — typos, spelling, broken formatting — and jump straight to each flagged moment.

## What makes it different

- **You direct; it edits.** Describe the result — *"cut a punchy 45-second short,"* *"clean up this talk"* — and it handles the cuts, timing, captions, and graphics.
- **Studied styles, not filters.** Its looks are measured from real, high-performing edits, so a "produced" short actually feels produced.
- **Your footage stays yours.** Video never leaves your machine — editing and rendering happen locally. (More in the [FAQ](faq.md).)
- **Finish like a pro.** Hand the exact result to Palmier Pro for final color and export whenever you want more control.
