This audits the exact P7 and P8 requirements in
[the implementation roadmap](07_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md#p7--directional-pilot).
It does not turn a fast renderer baseline or isolated phase demo into a
complete-project performance claim.

## Evidence rules

- A project counts only when one retained record binds its source, request,
  scope, plan, edits, outputs, QC, failures, clocks, and toolchain.
- Separate caption, scene, repair, render, and saga fixtures prove their own
  mechanisms; combining their test names does not create a completed project.
- The 90-minute clock starts at durable request acceptance and ends only at all
  selected approved outputs, QC, receipts, and Palmier readback.
- Failed, fallback, retried, and operator-intervention jobs stay in the
  denominator.

   destination, Palmier, asset, and reference-pack identities.
2. Pre-register S1–S3 and L1–L3, comparator assignment, organic-request
   classification, thresholds, blind review, failure denominator, and stop
   rules before observing outcomes.
3. Retain one complete authority graph per project, including every required
   repair, fallback, intervention, output, QC result, Palmier readback, and
   active-system/operator-wait clock.
4. Use the P7 results only for direction and variance. Freeze a separate
   release candidate and untouched P8 cohort without tuning on its outcomes.
5. Make cohort invalidation executable and fail closed on any frozen-identity
   change. Power any later latency-quantile or reliability claim separately.

> directional pilot performance, untouched confirmation, and the 90-minute
> `LF-14-A` product claim remain unqualified.
