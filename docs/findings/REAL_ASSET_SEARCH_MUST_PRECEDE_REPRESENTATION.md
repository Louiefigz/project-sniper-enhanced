# Search real assets before choosing a representation

## What failed

The September 15, 2026 pricing Short is 55.32 seconds long. Its diagrams explain
the intended pricing mechanism, but the strategy omitted real World Pet Health
and Skool material. A prior 5.6-second Skool capture already showed Trevor,
Pet Achievers, **11 members** and **$9/month**. Supplied Trevor footage and real
dog images were available too. The problem was not asset scarcity.

The authored request in
`artifacts/img7138-pricing-system-test-2026-09-15/author-plan.ts` contained:

```ts
mediaPolicy: { placement: "auto", sources: "local-only" }
```

That manually chosen restriction suppressed public scouting and excluded cached
public captures. The author reused an earlier Short's narrow asset factory and
justified a whole-program `no-insert` decision on the grounds that a community
page could not prove future business results. This applied the wrong test:
the page could establish the real business, offer and captured member count.
It did not need to prove the future $100k/month goal.

The previous critic reviewed the proposed graphics, source and references but
did not inspect the Skool candidate. Its pass covered that limited evidence.
The provenance identifies conversational authoring with local catalog use;
this was not an autonomous V9 Director model run.

## Why the system allowed it

- The normal request path preserves explicit `public-web`. No normalization
  bug was found. This manual plan supplied its own request without a prepared
  request binding.
- Existing structural checks validate the author's declared story jobs and
  asset decisions. A populated no-insert explanation can be structurally valid
  while overlooking a useful real source.
- Older instruction text said to choose the representation before scouting,
  preferred a HyperFrames composition by default, and allowed missing marks
  to become generic chips. Those defaults conflicted with real-first directing.
- Technical render checks cannot determine whether an omitted real asset would
  better establish the subject or substantiate the narration.

## Shared correction

The shared author, plan-review and rendered-review instructions now live in
[`visual-storytelling.ts`](../../src/lib/producer/visual-storytelling.ts).
Short direction uses the same standard. Producer and workflow instructions
require the following sequence for applicable produced work:

1. Resolve the current requested intensity, enabled lanes and source permission.
   Preserve authorized public scouting; do not copy an older local-only literal.
2. Identify the exact spoken subject and the viewer's information need. Inspect
   source footage, reusable assets and supplied prior feedback, then search
   authorized public sources for the real person, business, activity or detail.
3. Choose the asset or record a concrete rejection. Distinguish a real logo's
   identity job, a page's observable-state evidence, and a diagram's explanation
   of a mechanism or intended future state.
4. Have a separate strategy critic inspect the speech, actual candidates and
   proposed use/rejection before assembly. Missing scouting evidence, silently
   narrowed permission and blanket no-insert rationales require revision through
   the existing verdict contract.
5. At rendered review, check that the selected real asset appears on its spoken
   cue and that the relevant detail is readable. Keep internal provenance notes
   out of the frame unless a short qualification is needed for accurate meaning.

The Producer skill's conflicting HyperFrames-first hierarchy and generic-mark
fallback were replaced. HyperFrames is still the composition mechanism; it can
present and animate real assets. The same sourcing decision applies to both
Shorts and long-form videos.

The existing request writer freezes the accepted brief and permissions; see
[`NATIVE_SHORTS_WORKFLOW.md`](../producer/NATIVE_SHORTS_WORKFLOW.md#preserve-the-actual-request-before-choosing-assets).
Direct imports remain supported. Their internal consistency does not establish
agreement with a conversation unless authority is independently checked.

## When this does not mean adding B-roll

A deliberately simple presenter-led treatment can stay simple. Disabled lanes
remain disabled. An abstract mechanism may be clearest as an analogy or diagram.
A supplied-only job does not become public-web merely because relevant public
media was cached. A current page showing 25 members cannot be altered to prove
a historical count of 11. The selection is driven by the shot's job and current
authorization, not creator names or a quota of logos and screenshots.

Tool-free long-form critics cannot browse or open linked scouting reports.
The preparation owner must scout and embed relevant observations and claim
limits in the existing manifest and plan rationale fields. The critic flags
missing evidence rather than pretending it independently searched.

The independent reviewer found the two deeper Producer conflicts described
above. Both were corrected and re-reviewed; no material contradiction remained
in those sections or the new request-authority instructions. A separate agent
audited the request path, and another inspected actual assets and receipts.

Focused checks passed: short direction (5 tests), shared author prompts (4),
native Director/critic (5), native request preservation and media integrity

expanded to accept the existing earlier origin-integrity rejection; production
validation was not relaxed. Producer skill validation and `git diff --check`
passed.

These checks establish instruction integration, request integrity and the
observed agent decisions. They do not make editorial judgment deterministic or
certify an unrendered revision.
