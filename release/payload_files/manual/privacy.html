What leaves your Mac, and what never does

Checked against the code in this package, path by path. Read this before you edit anything confidential.

Your video file never leaves your Mac. No raw recording, no rendered video and no audio file is uploaded by the supported editing workflow. The code refuses to route video to a third party.

Text is not the only thing that leaves. During the visual review step, Sniper extracts still frames from the video it just rendered and sends those images to your provider so it can look at the result. That is how it catches a graphic in the wrong place or an unreadable caption. Still images of your footage do leave your machine. An earlier version of this manual said only text was sent. That was wrong, and this page is the correction.

What that means in practice

  • The words you said go to your provider. So do pictures of your finished frames.
  • Your provider's own data policy for your plan governs what happens to them. Sniper does not change it and cannot see it.
  • If a recording is confidential enough that a still frame of it must not be sent, do not run the graphics or visual-review lanes on it. A trim-only edit still sends the transcript.
  • The app itself binds to 127.0.0.1 only — it is not reachable from another machine on your network, and it has no login because nothing else can reach it.

Where your files live

~/ProjectSniper/<project>/
  source/     your footage and its transcripts
  producer/   edit_plan.json, base_final.mp4, final.mp4
  segments/   Segmenter exports
  clipper/    Clipper timelines

That folder is outside this package, so updating or moving the app never touches your projects. You can move it and point Sniper at the new location — see Licence, updates, backup and uninstall.
