## Apply the mechanism to a new story

**Use when:** How a command enters the system is part of the lesson.

**Avoid when:** The input is obvious and only the output needs attention.

**Preserve:**

- The input visibly develops rather than being replaced by a noun label.
- The specific request matches the eventual result.
- Keep the listening/key cue distinct from the content being entered.

**Layout and presenter:** Head for instruction, full composer for readable requirements, split for clarification, full dashboard for payoff.

**Pacing:** Reference’s typing action lasts about6.65seconds; preserve that kind of reading window when the requirement matters.
