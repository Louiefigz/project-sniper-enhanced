Project Sniper

Start here

A video editor that runs on your Mac. You describe the result in plain language; it cuts, captions, adds graphics and renders a finished video — and gives you the editable project behind it.

This copy is a release candidate, not a finished product. It has not passed its installation test on a clean Mac, its full-length and Short output tests, or its delivery test. The licence terms in this package are a draft and are not in force. Read RELEASE.json for this build's exact status, and PENDING-OWNER-DECISIONS.txt for what is still unsettled.

Three steps

  1. Check what you have. A Mac with Apple silicon, Node 22+, Python 3.12+, ffmpeg and whisper-cpp, and a Codex or Claude subscription you already pay for. The full list, with what costs money, is in What Sniper is.
  2. Install. Open Terminal in this folder and run install/install.command (or double-click it in Finder), then install/sign-in.command. Everything it installs stays inside this folder; your own Node, Python, Codex or Claude setup and login are not changed. Step-by-step: Install and first launch.
  3. Make your first edit. Your first three edits walks a short sample, then a full-length video, then a Short from your own recording.
