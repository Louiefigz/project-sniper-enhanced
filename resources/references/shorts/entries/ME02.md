# ME02 — List the items, then the total they make

Original reference written for Project Sniper and rendered from its own motion templates. Adapt the mechanism with the speaker's own words and footage; never reuse this copy or its numbers.

**Story job:** Make a total believable by showing the items it is made of.

**Use when:** The speaker adds up a handful of items and states the total.

**Do not use when:** The items are estimates shown as exact amounts, or there are more than six of them.
