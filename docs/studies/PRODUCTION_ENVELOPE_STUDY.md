## 1. The core finding — production is FRONT-LOADED

The entire production stack lives in the hook. The body breathes.

| | Intro / hook (~first 55–60s) | Body (60s → ~end) | Outro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut type | **hard punch-in cuts** every ~1.5–2s | long **10–30s holds** | one 30–68s hold |
| Motion | framing *snaps* wide↔tight (no ease) | slow **eased push-in creep** | settled |
| Graphics | stacked: chips, statement cards, credibility CARD, b-roll, flashes | rare — occasional card / cutaway | CTA only |
| Captions | NONE on long-form (words-on-screen is a SHORTS format element) | none | — |

**Earn the attention hard, then deliver calmly.** The first ~10% of runtime carries
the large majority of the technique; the remaining ~90% is mostly clean
talking-head punctuated occasionally.

### Measured envelope
- `angle generator` (654s): 52 hard scene-cuts total; the intro punch-in cadence
  (~1.5–2s framing jumps ≈ 30–40 framing-changes/min) is **not** visible to scene
  detection (same blue-wall background → sub-threshold), so a naive cuts/min
  average (4.8) badly understates the intro and overstates the body. The honest
  reading is the **distribution**: dense intro, long-hold body (median body hold
  ~12s, up to 68s), 68s sustained outro.
- `Replaced 5 tools` (975s): scene-cut density peaks 0–120s then tapers
  monotonically — same front-loaded shape.
- **Caveat baked in:** cuts/min as a single number lies here. Always report the
  envelope, never the mean.

## 2. The technique vocabulary (shared across BOTH edits — it's a system)

1. **Punch-in jump cuts** — same shot, framing snaps between a WIDER and a TIGHTER
   crop. Hard cut, no ease. The intro's primary energy source (no b-roll needed).
2. **Karaoke captions with color-coded emphasis** — key words burned in red /
   green / magenta while the rest is white.
3. **Corner icon animate-ins** — tool/concept marks pop into a top corner one by
   one, connected by a thin arc (ChatGPT → +Claude → +Gemini).
4. **Full-frame statement cards** — a rounded dark card, ONE sentence, the payload
   phrase bolded ("That line alone will fix half of your **content problems**").
   Full-frame takeover, not an overlay on the face.
5. **Credibility TAKEOVER** — the subject is shrunk into a **circular PIP** joined
   by a line to animated identity/claim text in magenta ("I'm the host" / "So I've spent a
   decade…"). Lands **~28–30s in BOTH videos** — a templated beat.
6. **B-roll cutaways** — full-frame stock illustrating a concept (man at a laptop).
7. **Color-flash transitions** — a brief light-leak / color wash (green) between beats.
8. **Emphasis graphics** — a glowing underline / circle drawn under a key phrase.

Reframe/graphics doctrine holds (feedback: *pro graphics are full-frame cutaways,
not panels-on-face*): even the credibility card repositions the person into a PIP
rather than slapping text on their face.

## 3. The two motion languages (the "alive" driver)

There is not one zoom behavior — there are two, by region:

- **Intro = hard punch-in CUTS.** The framing changes are *cuts*, not zooms — an
  instant snap between crops on speech beats. No easing. This reads as energy.
- **Body = slow eased PUSH-IN creep.** Each long hold carries a gentle continuous
  zoom so the frame is never frozen (measured: subject grows across a 30s hold).
  This is the existing `aliveness` creep — confirmed correct against the real edit.

Mixing them up is the tell of an amateur edit: eased creeps in the hook feel
sluggish; hard punch-cuts in the body feel jittery.

## 4. The intro template (repeatable sequence + timing)

Observed in both, in order:
1. Hook line — karaoke + color-emphasis, over rapid punch-in cuts.
2. Concept/tool **icon animate-ins** in a corner.
3. A **full-frame statement card** for the core claim.
4. The **credibility takeover** (PIP + name), ~28–30s.
5. Product/brand logo + colored caption.
6. First **b-roll cutaway**.
7. → settle into the body (long holds + eased creep).

## 5. What this means for the editor brain (the wiring — see §6)

- **Front-load the stack.** Concentrate graphics/cuts/zooms in the hook window;
  keep the body sparse. Enforced by the hook-front-load pacing gate
  (`hook_front_load`, `hook_density_multiplier`, `hook_window_s`).
- **Region-aware pacing.** The still-gap ceiling must be TIGHT in the hook and
  LOOSE in the body — a 30s hold is a defect in the intro and correct in the body.
- **Region-aware motion.** Punch-in *cuts* in the hook; eased *creep* in the body.
- **The intro template** is a scaffold the brain can follow, not improvise.
