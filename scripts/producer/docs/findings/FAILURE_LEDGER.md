# FAILURE LEDGER — the PRODUCER learning loop

here, and every row must name how the failure was ENCODED so it cannot recur
silently: a lint rule id, a test name, a LESSON-id in the Brain lessons
section below, and/or a style-doc reference. Rows are never edited except to
flip `status`; new defects append below the last row.

Machine interface: `scripts/producer/ledger_lessons.py`
(`parse_rows()` / `parse_lessons()`); the derived "known past failure modes"
section of `QC_CHECKLIST.md` regenerates via `ledger_lessons.py sync-checklist`.

## Brain lessons

- LESSON-001: Never author CTA or card copy the speaker does not say in the KEPT cut — if the spoken line sits outside the excerpt window, extend the cut to include it or drop the copy; structural chrome must be unspoken-neutral (no price, no promise).
- LESSON-002: Give every exit animation runway — end a blur-recede window at least 0.3s before the next cut seam, or set exitOnCut for a hard clear on the cut.
- LESSON-003: Land the first content element within 0.6s of an own-screen comp's entrance (0.9s for panels) — enter the card later (still word-locked) rather than opening on empty chrome; blank chrome reads as a stall.
- LESSON-004: Do not hold a left-column own-screen layout (whiteboard-list) past ~5s — shorten the hold, enter closer to the first item land, or pick a balanced comp; a half-empty canvas reads as unfinished.
- LESSON-005: Keep receipt/evidence lines legible at phone size — separator glyph between label and value, the brighter receipt gray, and relabel "CLAIM SOURCE" when the cited thing is not actually the claim's evidentiary source (e.g. "BUILDING" for the speaker's own product).
- LESSON-006: Check accent/text contrast against the comp's background token before authoring — large text needs at least 3:1; accent blue #054BC9 fails on the dark navy card, use a brighter accent there.
- LESSON-007: Zooms mark emphasis beats or serve recompose moves — never author a zoom purely to keep the screen alive; to break a longform still-gap, extend the adjacent panel's hold or add a graphic instead, and stamp every intentional punch with its trigger/evidence so the lint can tell emphasis from filler.
- LESSON-008: A slip-cover must show the subject actively DOING something (gesturing, working, moving) — silent staring reads as an outtake; score candidate windows with edit/cover_select.py (retake-adjacent and silent+static windows are hard-excluded), and when no candidate clears the floor, cover the seam with a graphic takeover instead.
- LESSON-009: A graphic must EARN its beat — either advance the argument (new structure, a receipt, the next point) or emphasize the exact words being spoken at its window; a card that merely restates the sentence verbatim, or decorates, is WORSE than clean face. The claims-contract coverage floor kills unrelated copy deterministically, but verbatim-redundant restatement passes it — before authoring any card, ask "what does the viewer get here that the audio alone doesn't", and cut the card if the answer is nothing.
- LESSON-010: Slip-cover footage must come from CLEAN kept-adjacent delivery, never from inter-take gaps — always pick via edit/cover_select.py (retake-adjacent, lip-flap and silent-static windows are hard-excluded) and never hand-place an assetStart; any VISIBLE mouthing in a cover is an AV-sync defect to the viewer (wrong lips read as broken audio even when the mux is sample-accurate), so keep spoken overlap ≤ COVER_SELECT.lip_flap_max_s.
