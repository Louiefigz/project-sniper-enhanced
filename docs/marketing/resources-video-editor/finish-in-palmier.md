# Finish & export in Palmier Pro

The editor renders a finished video on its own — but when you want to hand-polish, color-grade, or export through a real timeline, that's what **Palmier Pro** is for.

## How it works

- Keep **Palmier Pro open** while you work — your edit shows up there as you go.
- When you're happy with the AI's cut, send it to Palmier as a **finishing step**. Your exact approved video lands there as a single clip — frame-for-frame identical to what you approved.
- Do your final touches — color, manual tweaks, export — with full manual control.

## Good to know

- The AI's render is the **source of truth**. Palmier is where you *finish*, not where the edit is rebuilt from scratch.
- Graphics, motion, and transitions arrive baked into that video exactly as approved.
- Palmier is **optional** — if the AI's finished video is all you need, you're already done.
