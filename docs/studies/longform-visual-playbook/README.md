This extends the existing Shorts research library with landscape references.
`LF01` studies the operator's [Jack Roberts reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_MrumlZnGic):

## The two explicit priorities

**The opening must earn attention.** The operator specifically liked it. The
reference begins with its title already readable over a dark hero, bright blue
light and an emblem, with the presenter visible. Around 10.1s it previews a
finished design; around 16.25s it previews a business dashboard. It returns to
the premise at about 18.5s, scrolls to an explanatory diagram around 20s, and
moves to the actual interface around 39s. The body later delivers those previews.
Preserve the concrete promise, visual contrast and payoff. A slow generic title
entrance would change this directing choice.

**Screen share carries the body.** A rounded browser occupies about 91% of the
frame width. The rounded-square presenter inset occupies about 23% of frame
width / 40% of height at bottom left. It persists until the change to full
presenter between the inspected 912.933s and 913.017s frames. These are manual
reference measurements, not mandatory geometry for another UI. Dense content
sometimes sits behind the inset; reposition or hide it if our evidence needs
that space.

Most of the animation belongs to the captured pages: hero effects, slide changes,
charts, graphs, dashboards and scrolling. Capture our own actual demonstration.
A browser-shell component cannot supply the missing interaction or result.
Do not reconstruct the whole app as animation merely because it looks polished.

## What the full chronology preserves

The case contains 23 contiguous editorial sections, including the opening,
setup, comparisons, three-topic roadmap, design examples, midroll offer, business
data examples, application interaction, economics, limitations, visual callback
and presenter closing. Each records source interval, spoken context in paraphrase,
visible behavior, viewer purpose, frame evidence and reusable catalog candidates.
Section boundaries are approximate; they are not a frame-accurate EDL.

The [state timeline](../../../artifacts/longform-reference-study-2026-09-16/state-timeline.json)
retains all 222 deduplicated visual states. Each state points to a full frame,
its hash, its editorial section and candidate tags. A state's filename/time marks
the beginning of a run; its representative may occur later within that run.
The [native-frame manifest](../../../artifacts/longform-reference-study-2026-09-16/native-frame-evidence.json)
provides actual source frame indices and decoded PTS for 101 additional selected
frames. It includes the opening changes, mechanism scroll, two page transitions,
body examples and the final presenter cut.

The title is already on-screen at frame zero. Native bursts distinguish clean
tab changes from scrolling within the page. Around 341.6s, two slide pages overlap
in a crossfade while the browser and presenter remain. At the closing change,
the sampled boundary shows a hard cut. Do not add a whole-frame transition to
all three situations. Preserve native motion already in the recording.

The 29 scene-change candidates, 1.88 candidates/minute and 222 visual states are
different measurements. A page can scroll through many states with no editorial
cut. A diagram can remain while the explanation develops. None of these numbers
is a universal long-form cadence or proof of attention/retention performance.

The completed deep pass retains 1,324 motion events and 16 unclassified runs at
native 60 fps. Research mode reads text from all 222 states, while explicitly
leaving per-event graphic text timing and the separate caption OCR pass
unmeasured. The default full OCR mode remains unchanged.

## Saved component choices

Eight exact catalog sources were inspected against the reference images and
selected motion sequences. The case retains source SHA-256, observed behavior,
configuration, specific mismatch and playback status for each.

The reusable records are saved as `LF01-M01` through `LF01-M08` beside the
canonical study and in the artifact's `reference_catalog_matches.json`.
`SAVED-MATCHES-CHECK.json` verifies 399 evidence inputs. All eight retain explicit
production prerequisites; the title-card comparison records a capability gap.
The reference-only strategy handoff freezes 49 documents containing all 39
references, all 418 catalog records, the selected study and these saved matches.
It is in the artifact's `strategy-reference-inputs/` directory and makes no
claim that an original episode strategy has been authored.

A catalog source inspection does not grant native execution or design approval.
Saved research can retain explicit adapter/asset blockers without being discarded;
`check-study` reports them. Reusing a blocked match in a production map remains
blocked until the new project resolves its prerequisites. A plain footage shot
may require no graphic at all.

If a future episode needs a new beam, glass card or other missing mechanism,
keep the existing usable pieces and build only the evidenced gap. New work belongs
in a separate experimental library with preview, defects and test evidence until
both the user wants the design and technical qualification passes. This case
creates no custom component and promotes none.

## Applying this to our own 10–15 minute video

1. Ingest our narration, presenter recording and screen capture through the normal
   admitted-source workflow. Keep genuine actions/results available; record pickups
   for missing demonstrations.
2. Select the reference in long-form mode and prepare the local strategy packet.
   The complete indexes and this case make existing choices visible immediately.
3. Write the whole-video structure first: opening promise, previewed payoffs,
   sections, evidence, limitations, callback and ending. Use the new episode's
   actual content; a vlog may need a different format entirely.
4. Plan each source-bound shot with the screen content, presenter position,
   focus/readability need, timing, catalog choice and adjacent-shot handoff.
   Reuse saved matches before new discovery. Timing follows the new speech.
5. Assemble through the existing native route, then review actual motion, joins,
   seeking/restart, repeated instances, full playback and encoded output. Deliver
   both local playback and the matching editable HyperFrames Studio project.

The reference alone is sufficient for this study and library entry. A finished
original strategy/render needs our episode's topic, content and admitted media;
those were not supplied in this request.
