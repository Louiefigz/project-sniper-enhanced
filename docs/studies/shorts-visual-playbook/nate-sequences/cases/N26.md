**Lesson:** A buyer evaluates the destination and business outcome; the technology is the means of getting there.

**Opening:** The presenter names three business priorities, promising a simpler way to sell AI work. This is a spoken opening carried by a face; the early money badge is partly clipped and is not a strong standalone visual hook.

**Story:** State buyer priorities → demonstrate destination versus interchangeable vehicles → return to interpret the analogy → reject the technical feature pitch → visualize leads without ads → restate the outcome rule.

**Payoff:** The taxi analogy earns the outcome-over-tool lesson. The bot and envelopes illustrate a proposed service; they do not establish qualified leads or customer results.

**Why study this case:** Adds a coherent multi-picture analogy, a negative example, and an evolving system scene. It also shows why long presenter holds can coexist with fast visual runs. N30 reuses the outreach scene, so it is not counted as independent evidence here.

**Review limits:** Complete chronological 0.5-second source-frame sheets and machine-timed transcript inspected; listed native strips refine selected motions. No audio audition or uninterrupted realtime playback. Image-to-words reasoning is editorial interpretation, not known creator intent or retention evidence.
