## What the reference does

Two video regions stack nearly equally: original person above, alternate output below. Both show moving faces/gestures; the caption sits across their boundary.

**Type and hierarchy:** White phrase captions with occasional turquoise emphasis and dark edge; two media panes supply the information. A caption treatment is independent of the split mechanism.

**Timing:** The sequence shows selected source states; inspect the original interval for exact motion, easing and spoken synchronization.

**Words and story:** The visible captions link the alternate output to the claim that it is a digital twin and moves like the source person. This is a paraphrase of the relevant passage/captions, not a verbatim or phonetic transcript.

**Why this treatment helps:** Let the viewer compare an original person and a digital-twin output while the claim is made.

## Apply the mechanism to a new story

**Use when:** The viewer benefits from inspecting two corresponding states simultaneously.

**Avoid when:** Either pane becomes unreadable, or only one pane provides information.

**Preserve:**

- Two large readable media regions, each with a clear reason to be present.
- Compare corresponding source/output material, not unrelated B-roll.
- Keep identifying faces and meaningful gestures inside both crops.

**Layout and presenter:** Head full for assertion; top evidence/bottom head where both can fit; full evidence when text or comparison needs most of the frame.

**Pacing:** Hold through the visible action plus a readable settled result; references include four-second comparisons and five-second conversations.

**Return to the person:** On interpretation, qualification or next question, after the result has been read.

**Get these materials first:** Use the retained speaker footage and obtain the actual result screen/video before designing the split. Inspect face and hand bounds on source frames; author a tracked crop only where source movement requires it. Record evidence at enough resolution to read the target at final portrait size.

**Improve this limit:** Face/head tracking is a separate asset-analysis/crop task; a zoom catalog item does not perform it. A screen skeleton is not a product demonstration.

**Do not transfer:**

- Do not shrink the evidence merely to retain a presenter badge.
- Do not add a decorative wipe when simultaneous comparison is the point.
