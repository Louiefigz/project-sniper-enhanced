# ME03 — Mark the start of a new part

Original reference written for Project Sniper and rendered from its own motion templates. Adapt the mechanism with the speaker's own words and footage; never reuse this copy or its numbers.

**Story job:** Tell the viewer the Short has moved on to its next part.

**Use when:** The speaker clearly moves from one part to the next, for example from the problem to the fix.

**Do not use when:** The Short has only one part, or the marker would cover the speaker's face.

Composition: section-marker
