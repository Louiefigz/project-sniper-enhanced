## Pacing and storytelling

There is no universal one-second or three-second cut rule. The request and
retained script drive the overall tempo, while each beat declares its attention
target, change reason and reading/action hold. Fast passages can support quicker
changes; a meaningful result or a deliberate pause needs enough time to read.
The same subject should visibly develop through setup, consequence and payoff.

The offer uses one developing offer and a final 1.24-second payoff beat; the real
feeding video occupies 2.8 seconds and hands off to a matching source still.
The Claude example uses 5.2 seconds of actual page movement, then tracks the same
client video through the listed tools. Structural story/pacing reports require
these obligations; they do not prove that a finished edit feels engaging.
