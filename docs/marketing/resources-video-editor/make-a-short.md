# Make a short

Vertical, punchy, ready for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts — from a longer recording or a raw clip.

## How to do it

Point the editor at your footage and ask for a short, or pick **Short (9:16)** on the intent card. Tell it the vibe (or pick a style below) and it will:

- cut the dead air, filler, and flubbed takes
- tighten the pacing
- apply bounded vertical framing: select a detected face when available, then
  fall back to center-crop or blur-pad
- burn in karaoke-style captions
- add a hook card and graphics where they earn their place

Vertical framing is evaluated in bounded windows; it is not continuous subject
tracking. Review shots with multiple faces, fast movement, occlusion, or a
speaker leaving frame.

## Pick a style

Three studied short-form looks (plus neutral presets):

| Style | Feels like |
|---|---|
| **Clean** *(Caleb light)* | Restrained and confident — few cuts, no gimmicks, the words carry it. |
| **Produced** *(Jaden produced)* | Punchy and kinetic — breath-gap cuts, layered captions, high energy. |
| **Involved** *(Angela involved)* | Slide-driven — bold on-screen graphics and takeover cards. |

Not sure? Start with **Clean** for talking-head clarity or **Produced** for scroll-stopping energy.

## Want several shorts from one recording?

Ask for *"the best 3 shorts from this recording"* or *"make the strongest short
from this finished long video."* Producer compares moments across the whole
source for a clear standalone hook, useful evidence and a complete payoff, then
trims the selected ranges and builds each short. Topic boundaries are only a
navigation aid; you do not need to export rough clips first.

For a produced short, the adopted student-kit storytelling guidance connects
what the opening promises to what the viewer sees and learns. Graphics develop
the idea, captions follow the kept speech, and the layout is composed for a
phone. Existing burned-in text and music are accounted for during repurposing.
Selection is editorial judgment, not a prediction of views. Complete quality
and turnaround still need verification on the actual short.

See [the complete workflow](../../producer/PRODUCER_README.md#long-form-to-finished-shorts).

## Just a clean cut

Only want the silences and bad takes gone — no graphics? Ask for a **clean cut**. Same tight edit, none of the production layers.
