## Apply the mechanism to a new story

**Use when:** An available authentic image directly supports the experience being described.

**Avoid when:** Only generic stock imagery is available or the image would imply unproven results.

**Preserve:**

- A relevant real asset appears on the experience claim.
- It occupies enough space to recognize the scene.
- The insert clears so the explanation returns to the speaker.

**Layout and presenter:** One large photo over the presenter for a bounded interval, then return to the clear talking shot.

**Pacing:** Set entrance, reading hold and removal from the new retained speech; reference duration is an observation, not a preset.

**Return to the person:** Keep the person when expression or explanation is the evidence; return after an insert has done its specific job.

**Get these materials first:** Use the retained owned presenter footage and new source-supported copy; acquire any real supporting image before this scene is assembled.

**Improve this limit:** A consultation photo does not prove the exact client count or business result.

**Do not transfer:**

- Do not borrow the creator's photo for our own claim.
- A consultation photo does not prove the exact client count or business result.
