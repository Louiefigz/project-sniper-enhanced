The useful distinction is how much explanation the picture must carry. Personal
judgment and experience often benefit from preserving the person; operations,
comparisons and transformations often need a visible example. Either treatment
can use multiple views. A different card every two seconds is not the goal.

## How this changes our directing decisions

1. **Select the complete thought.** The shortlist needs the problem, turn,
   qualification and useful payoff. Present playable moments and the reason each
   is strong before polishing the chosen short. Reuse source transcription.
2. **Choose the treatment from that thought.** Record edit intensity separately
   from the viewing layout. Preserve personal delivery when it carries conviction
   or nuance; make the explanatory object prominent when understanding depends
   on seeing a change. A short can move between these treatments.
3. **Build the opening from its answer.** Retrieve relevant YouTube title formulas
   and compare distinct, source-supported openings. Adapt a formula's attention
   mechanism into concise screen copy; do not paste a long title or invent
   recorded speech. The written hook, actual spoken opener and first visual must
   open the same question. Identify why the viewer should care and where the
   retained footage delivers the promise.
4. **Write what visibly happens on each cue.** Name the object, action, changed
   result, view and readable hold. A speaker can be the necessary subject; for a
   process, naming steps is insufficient. Keep the same object identifiable
   through the operation. Use a full visual when detail needs the space. Use
   split only when both panes have a job and the evidence remains readable.
5. **Resolve assets and the mechanism before assembly.** Specify the exact source
   footage, website interaction/result or original explanatory scene. Obtain and
   inspect it, including its portrait crop. Match an existing catalog mechanism
   to that need. Document any real gap before custom work. A skeleton browser,
   unnamed B-roll placeholder or generic animation is not an acquired asset.
6. **Plan pace within and between views.** A selection, cut, reveal or result may
   renew attention inside one scene. Faster passages need useful developments;
   an action or thoughtful delivery may need a longer hold. Caption updates,
   physical gestures, reframes and new evidence are different events. Do not
   count them all as new templates or force authentic passages onto a two-second
   replacement clock.
7. **Review the actual native Studio sequence.** Keep footage, captions and
   graphics editable. Check opening readability, word-linked action, result hold,
   caption boundaries, face/gesture clearance and the payoff at phone size with
   audio. Resolve uncertain mechanics in a small preview before full assembly.
   Revisions reuse cuts, transcription and unaffected assets/scenes; final
   playback QA still applies.

## Catalog choices with known limits

The current registry inventory has 399 installable items. This is a supply of
mechanisms, not 399 completed scenes. The audit inspected actual source for the
following candidates; no new portrait build has been qualified in this study.

| Needed job | Closest inspected candidate | Required planning decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restrained opening proposition | `titlecard-calm`, or the existing static native title | Choose a safe host rectangle, font, words and removal cue. The calm title's default headline starts hidden until 0.28s and then animates; it does not satisfy immediate visibility unchanged. |
| Subtle entrance on existing text | `soft-blur-in` | Use only if the reveal helps. It supplies no typography, caption scheduler or exit. A static title may fit better. |
| One actual belief change | `line-swap` | Supply the two supported propositions and exact turn. Long text shrinks in its single-line layout; it is not an arbitrary paragraph container. |
| Ordinary speech captions | Existing working timed-caption layer; `caption-weight-shift` only if its behavior is needed | The catalog sample is fixed landscape with a demo transcript. Portrait reflow and actual word clocks are necessary. Reusing our working layer is less work than creating another caption system. |
| Real before/after evidence | `before-after-wipe` | Supply corresponding authentic assets; choose enough space to see the difference. Default placeholder panels are not evidence. |
| Website action and detail | `browser-device-stage` + `ui-focus-zoom`; selective highlight | Capture the actual action/result first. Crop around the relevant detail; preserve continuity between input and output. A browser frame does not supply a demonstration. |
| A causal process | Existing process/connector components composed with actual state changes | `hw-pipeline` and `flowchart-vertical` reveal structures but do not animate arbitrary editing operations. Supply the semantic scene; do not substitute their labels for the process. |

The reuse order is **reuse → configure → compose existing → bounded custom for
an evidenced gap**. A close functional match is sufficient. Exact imitation of
a reference's font, color or easing is not a reason to replace working motion.
Font names declared in catalog sources do not identify the fonts in a reference.

For the five-step video-editing example, show one filmstrip being shortened,
reordered, reframed, supplemented and mixed, if those are the actual script's
steps. Preserve its identity and show each consequence. HyperFrames can author
that deterministic sequence; catalog primitives help with framing, emphasis and
transitions. The stateful editing illustration is custom scene content where
needed. Higgsfield is unnecessary for this example. A three-second introductory
sentence may only permit a journey preview; the explanation needs adequate
narration and holds to demonstrate five unfamiliar operations.

## Scope correction to earlier doctrine

The July `CALEB_STYLE.md` describes a different three-reel cohort. Its zero-zoom,
caption-only findings must not become a creator-wide prohibition: the September
sample includes repeated crop changes and an actual framework card. Likewise,
earlier continuous-hook-shot and fixed hook-stack examples are not universal.
Keep old measured jobs and configurations intact; select the current treatment
from the new source and brief. None of these samples establishes optimal cadence.

## What changed and what remains implementation work

The Producer skill now requires style-fit decisions, catalog reuse before
custom, a hook attention reason and removal cue, and conditional pacing.
Producer-study and reference-editor now distinguish close-match research from
the stricter compiled style-pack route; ordinary research no longer implies
building replicas or converting small-sample timing observations into constants.

The automatic Shorts planner still needs the connections documented in
`artifacts/nate-herk-30-shorts-study-2026-09-10/IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md`:
title-formula retrieval, semantic visual directing, asset acquisition, native
assembly and corresponding checks. Skill instructions are not those application
changes. We should prove one complete source-to-Studio short with visual/audio
review before calling the automatic workflow optimized.
