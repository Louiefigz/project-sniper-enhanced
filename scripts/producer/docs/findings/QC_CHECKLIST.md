## Standing checks (run all, adversarial default: WARN unless proven clean)

1. **Graphic entrances/exits vs the plan** — burst-sample each
   `graphicsTrack` entry's entrance and exit at 12fps (24fps micro-bursts on
   suspicion): entrances land composed (no half-rendered state), exits clear
   fully ON their seam or complete their animation BEFORE it; word-locked
   lands hit their words within ~1 frame.
2. **Text inside frame + readable** — every card/rail/takeover/list frame:
   no clipped glyphs, no off-canvas text, contrast legible at phone size
   (crop-check the dimmest text), caption config honored (burn flag,
   sidecar present).
3. **Presenter presence + PIP integrity** — face visible/sharp/framed in
   every takeover hole across entrance/settle/steady/exit bursts; rings and
   rounded masks intact; rails never cover the face; slip-covers read as
   deliberate angles; no face-absent frames at 12fps sampling.
4. **Factual claims grounded in the SPOKEN cut** — every rendered
   number AND phrase in cards/lists/takeovers must be spoken inside the
   kept cut (verify against the source transcript mapped through the
   cutTrack — NOT captions.srt, which is display-paced); receipts cite real
   sources; labels don't misattribute.
5. **Adversarial seam/dead-frame sweep** — YDIF dup scan over the whole
   render: no footage freezes, no dead frames beyond deliberate 2-frame
   swaps; seam covers (flash/leak) overlap their cuts; punch steps land on
   word onsets or breath gaps; own-screen card holds may be pixel-frozen by
   grammar, their ENTRANCES may not.
6. **Rendered audio, on the actual delivery artifact** — require −14±1 LUFS,
   true peak below −1 dBTP, matched audio/video endpoints, balanced live
   channels, and no sustained narrow 40–240 Hz tone. With dialogue, music must
   stay below voice and fade cleanly; reject a music-only tail. SFX should be
   absent by default and sparse/non-distracting when explicitly justified.
