## Apply the mechanism to a new story

**Use when:** The lesson is about several choices within one consistent category.

**Avoid when:** The entire proposition or causal relationship changes.

**Preserve:**

- Stable grammatical frame and one changing choice.
- Only the option slot changes; surrounding meaning stays clear.
- Leave each necessary option readable.

**Layout and presenter:** Type above head for breadth; full or large crop of actual menu for selection.

**Pacing:** Names may cycle quickly, but hold the real menu long enough to find the option being recommended.

**Return to the person:** At the recommendation of which option to choose and why.

**Get these materials first:** Inspect the available model menu in the actual target app. Save a legible capture and use only the options present for the account/workspace being explained.

**Improve this limit:** Typographic brand names establish identity; they do not prove access, model quality or account eligibility.

**Do not transfer:**

- Do not rotate unrelated slogans to simulate variety.
- Do not show outdated or unavailable choices as current capabilities.
