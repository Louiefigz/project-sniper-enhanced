# Director training pairs — problem-aware viewers

This viewer knows something is wrong but not how to fix it. Each pair walks
one illustrative recording through the Director's whole decision: format, anchor,
slot bindings, the chosen written opening (`Output`), a rejected fill and the six
opening criteria (see `README.md`). Recordings and numbers were written for
Project Sniper on 2026-09-18 as teaching material; they are not real people or
real results. Never reuse an output as copy.

### T701

- Viewer start: problem_aware — their loaves come out dense and they do not know why.
- Recording: "If your bread comes out dense, it's usually under-proofed. Give it another hour, then press it: the dent should spring back slowly."
- Format: `single_adjustment`. Rejected `ordered_steps`: the press test is a check, not a second step.
- Anchor: `situation-if-you`, using this entry's formula. Rejected `gap-cause-behind` with R504: the speaker answers in the first sentence, so a question would delay an answer the recording gives at once.
- Slots: thing ← "bread"; symptom ← "dense"; cause ← "under-proofed".
- Output: "If your bread comes out dense, it's usually under-proofed"
- Rejected fill: "Dense bread? Here's the fix" — no concrete detail, and "the fix" promises more than one extra hour of proofing.
- Criteria: supportedClaim keeps the speaker's "usually"; answerablePromise is answered by the press test; viewerStake is the dense loaf; concreteDetail is "under-proofed"; channelAgreement needs the first picture to show the loaf or the presenter holding it; glanceReadable is nine words on two lines.

### T702

- Viewer start: problem_aware — they squeeze their tyres and believe the pressure is fine.
- Recording: "The tyre felt hard when I squeezed it, but the gauge said forty psi instead of sixty. A squeeze can't tell you the pressure; check with a gauge every week."
- Format: `error_correction`. Rejected `single_adjustment`: trusting the squeeze is the lesson, not only the weekly gauge.
- Anchor: `mistake-looks-right`. Rejected `number-took-not` with R513: sixty is the tyre's rating, not something the speaker expected.
- Slots: thing ← "The tyre"; problem ← "the gauge said forty psi instead of sixty", written as "reads 40 psi, not 60".
- Output: "The tyre looks right but reads 40 psi, not 60"
- Rejected fill: "Your squeeze test is lying to you" — attributes intent to a test and drops both numbers.
- Criteria: supportedClaim uses only the two spoken figures; answerablePromise is answered by "check with a gauge every week"; viewerStake is a rider who squeeze-tests; concreteDetail is 40 against 60; channelAgreement needs the gauge reading in the first picture when the footage has it; glanceReadable is ten words.

### T703

- Viewer start: problem_aware — their coffee tastes sour.
- Recording: "Why does my coffee taste sour? The grind's too coarse, so the water runs through before it pulls out the sweeter part."
- Format: `error_correction`. Rejected `plain_definition`: no term is being defined.
- Anchor: `gap-cause-behind`. Rejected `assumption-more-wont` with R519: the recording never discusses adding more coffee.
- Slots: thing ← "my coffee", written as "your coffee"; observation ← "taste sour".
- Output: "Why does your coffee taste sour?"
- Rejected fill: "The secret to sweeter coffee" — "secret" overstates a common adjustment and names no symptom.
- Criteria: supportedClaim makes no claim beyond the question; answerablePromise is answered by the grind explanation; viewerStake is the sour cup; concreteDetail is "sour"; channelAgreement pairs the spoken question with the same written question; glanceReadable is six words.

### T704

- Viewer start: problem_aware — commuting feels costly but abstract.
- Recording: "Forty minutes of commuting a day is over a hundred and fifty hours a year, once you count working days."
- Format: `worked_numbers`. Rejected `assumption_check`: no belief is contradicted.
- Anchor: `number-adds-up`, using this entry's formula. Rejected `number-the-one`: two figures carry the point, not one.
- Slots: daily amount ← "Forty minutes"; yearly total ← "over a hundred and fifty hours", written as "150+ hours".
- Output: "40 minutes a day is 150+ hours a year"
- Rejected fill: "Your commute is stealing your life" — invents a stake the speaker did not state.
- Criteria: supportedClaim keeps "over" as "+" and the working-day basis goes in the payoff; answerablePromise is answered by the arithmetic; viewerStake is the viewer's own commute; concreteDetail is both numbers; channelAgreement needs the count to reach the spoken total on its cue; glanceReadable is eight words.

### T705

- Viewer start: problem_aware — the home network has dead zones.
- Recording: "Before you buy a new router, look at where the old one sits. Mine was on the floor inside a cupboard; moving it onto a shelf fixed the dead zones upstairs."
- Format: `single_adjustment`. Rejected `side_by_side`: the recording does not compare routers.
- Anchor: `situation-before-you`. Rejected `moment-what-changed`: the lesson is the check, not the story.
- Slots: action ← "buy a new router", written as "buy a router"; thing ← "where the old one sits".
- Output: "Before you buy a router, check where the old one sits"
- Rejected fill: "Fix your Wi-Fi for free" — a general promise; the speaker describes one house.
- Criteria: supportedClaim limits the result to the speaker's house; answerablePromise is answered by the move to a shelf; viewerStake is money not yet spent; concreteDetail is the cupboard floor; channelAgreement needs the router in its old spot as the first picture if it was filmed; glanceReadable is eleven words on two lines.

### T706

- Recording: "The first time I presented without slides, people asked far more questions. I think they finally had something to react to: me."
- Format: `lived_moment`. Rejected `assumption_check`: the speaker does not claim that slides are bad in general.
- Anchor: `moment-first-time`. Rejected `number-took-not`: no figures were counted.
- Slots: action ← "presented without slides"; what happened ← "people asked far more questions", written as "people asked more".
- Output: "The first time I presented without slides, people asked more"
- Rejected fill: "Slides are killing your talks" — a general claim the recording does not make.
- Criteria: supportedClaim keeps it first-person and uncounted; answerablePromise is answered by the speaker's reason; viewerStake is a presenter who wants questions; concreteDetail is "without slides"; channelAgreement opens on the presenter, which matches the story; glanceReadable is ten words on two lines.
