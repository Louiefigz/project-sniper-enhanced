**Lesson:** Diagnose a measurable business problem, calculate outcome value, then price the solution in relation to that value.

**Opening:** The opening immediately begins a worked pricing example. A presenter and developing arithmetic establish a useful calculation, rather than an unrelated title card.

**Story:** Diagnose lost time → show automation and annual value → compare the price with that value → use a contractor analogy to contrast tools with outcomes → show a promised gain and shorter time → return to a practical discovery-call behavior.

**Payoff:** The visual argument connects value to price and gives example diagnostic questions. The side calculation and ending are cropped; transfer the method with all operands and units legible and a complete closing thought.

**Why study this case:** Adds cumulative calculation, simultaneous value/price comparison, a multi-object negative example, a number animation, a shortening duration bar and a simulated dialogue. These supply mechanisms beyond product screenshots.

**Review limits:** Complete chronological 0.5-second source-frame sheets and machine-timed transcript inspected; listed native strips refine selected motions. No audio audition or uninterrupted realtime playback. Image-to-words reasoning is editorial interpretation, not known creator intent or retention evidence.
