# Edit a long-form video

For 16:9, YouTube-style videos — where **you** choose exactly how much production you want.

## The checklist approach

Pick **Long (16:9)** and check the boxes for what you want done. Run the full workflow, or just the pieces you need:

- **Motion** — tasteful zooms and movement so the frame never feels frozen.
- **Graphics** — on-screen cards, lower-thirds, and callouts where they help.
- **Transitions** — covers for energy cuts and montages.
- **Captions** — clean, readable subtitles.
- **B-roll** — supporting footage over the right beats.
- **Credibility** — cards that back up your claims and numbers.
- **Music & audio cleanup** — a bed with automatic ducking, plus dialogue enhancement and a broadcast-level audio master.

Leave the rest unchecked and it won't touch them.

## What you get

A finished cut with breathing-room pacing, chapter markers, and a subtitle file alongside the video — ready to upload.

## Honest by design

Every claim-bearing card is checked against what you **actually said**, so numbers and facts on screen match your words. And if the editor can't do something you asked for, it tells you — it won't fake it.
